How we behave on site

The five things the crew is briefed on.

Written into the crew brief, not left to chance — and the reason the same technician who diagnoses does the work.

  1. Knock, then floor protection. Drop cloths from the door to the work area, and they come back out with us.
  2. Show, then tell. The failed part, the camera video or the meter reading goes in front of the customer before any recommendation.
  3. Price changes need permission. If the job turns out to be bigger, nobody proceeds until the customer has agreed to the new number.
  4. Clean like a guest. Swept, tools out, old unit and packaging gone from the house.
  5. Follow-up is not optional. The owner calls back on the big jobs — repipes, replacements, sewer work — to check it is doing what it should.

Ask for the diagnosis in writing

On any Alfa visit you get what failed, what was replaced and what it costs, before the truck leaves. If you cannot get that from a quote, that is the answer.
