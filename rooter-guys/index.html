How do I know if I have a water leak? +
Common signs are a water bill that has gone up since winter, or an abnormally wet area on your property. A small water leak can quietly cost you money every month. A leak test and water line inspection can confirm it — we often find small leaks while doing other plumbing excavation repairs.
When should I call a plumber for a clogged drain? +
Slow drainage, gurgling noises when draining, and foul odors are signs of a clog. If a plunger doesn't clear it — or the clog keeps coming back — call before it gets worse. Persistent clogs usually do.
