Emergency Care

Seen fast when it hurts — we strive to handle emergencies within 1–2 business days.

After hours, Dr. Fruitman does her best to respond to dental emergencies herself, or direct them to the closest dental emergency clinic. Common dental emergencies: • Severe toothache or dental pain • Knocked-out or broken teeth • Lost fillings or crowns • Dental abscess or swelling What to do in a dental emergency: 1. Call our office at (416) 789-0705 2. For a knocked-out tooth, keep it moist in milk 3. Rinse with warm salt water for pain relief 4. Apply a cold compress to reduce swelling Dr. Fruitman has over 25 years of experience handling emergencies quickly and compassionately.

Restorative & Preventive Dentistry

Proactive prevention and education, so small problems never become big ones.

Preventive services: • Comprehensive dental examinations • Professional cleanings and polishings • Digital X-rays (low radiation) • Fluoride treatments • Dental sealants • Oral cancer screenings Restorative services: • Tooth-coloured fillings • Crowns and bridges • Root canal therapy • Dental implants • Dentures and partial dentures • Dental bonding Dr. Fruitman takes a minimally invasive approach, preserving as much natural tooth structure as possible.

Family Dentistry

One dentist for your whole family — because good early visits build lifelong habits.

Family dentistry means: • One dentist for your entire family • Personalized treatment plans for every age • Warm, welcoming environment • Flexible scheduling for busy families • Education on at-home dental care Dr. Fruitman has been serving families in mid-town Toronto for over 25 years. Her years as a camp counselor, math tutor, and certified ski instructor enhance her ability to connect with patients of every age.

👶

Children's Dentistry

Gentle, unhurried visits that help children feel at ease in the chair.

Children's dental care includes: • First dental visit (recommended by age 1) • Gentle examinations and cleanings • Fluoride treatments • Dental sealants • Tooth-coloured fillings for baby teeth Tips for parents: • Start brushing gums with a damp cloth before teeth appear • Schedule first dental visit by first birthday • Do not put child to bed with a bottle • Supervise brushing until age 7 Dr. Fruitman uses distraction as her secret weapon with children, believing sedation is best reserved for specialists.

Meet Your Dentist

Dr. Lisa Fruitman

DDS, University of Toronto

Dr. Fruitman obtained her DDS from the University of Toronto in 1989. Following in the footsteps of her father, a dentist himself, she was inspired by seeing how satisfied his patients were.

She worked alongside her father before opening her own practice in 1999, and has served the mid-town community for over 25 years. Outside the office she skis, plays tennis and squash, reads, cooks and teaches — passions that shape how she connects with patients of every age.
