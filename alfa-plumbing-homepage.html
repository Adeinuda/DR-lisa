Quick answers · also written for AI search

Frequently asked plumbing questions.

Short, factual answers to what people ask a plumber before they call one — every answer is drawn from what Alfa already publishes, so it stays true on the page and in the structured data.

What plumbing services does Alfa Plumbing Services provide?
Plumbing repair, toilet and faucet repair, drain cleaning, sewer line repair, replacement, installation and camera inspection, septic systems, water heater repair, installation, maintenance and tankless conversions, gas line repair and installation, CO detector installation, underground water line repair and leak detection, whole-house repiping, well water and filtration systems, remodel rough-ins, new construction, commercial plumbing, and emergency service.
Is Alfa licensed and insured?
Yes — the company publishes that it is a full-service licensed Texas Master Plumber business, licensed and insured, and states its policy pays for work-related bodily injury or property damage on your property so you aren’t left responsible if a worker is hurt. Confirm before launch: the current site shows two different licence numbers (MPL 36649 on one page, MPL 41752 on another) — only one can be correct.
Does Alfa offer emergency plumbing?
Yes. Call the office and a service technician is dispatched; the company’s published wording is “we’ll send a service technician right away.” Reviews describe urgent water heater work being handled late in the evening. Burst pipe, failed tank or sewage backing up: shut the water at the main and call.
How quickly can I get service?
Jobs are scheduled in the order they arrive — Alfa states it does not take offers to jump the queue. Most calls get help the same day; larger installs (for example a permitted septic system) run on a longer timeline because drawings and county approval come first, which the company puts at roughly a month.
What areas around Baytown does Alfa serve?
Baytown, La Porte, Channelview, Deer Park, Cloverleaf, Seabrook, Pasadena, Galena Park and Jacinto City, plus the Houston area and smaller surrounding communities including Pearland and Highlands.
How do I know whether my water heater needs repair or replacement?
Start with the age. Storage tanks generally last 10–15 years, so if the unit is past roughly 7 years, replacement is often the better buy. Before that, have the parts tested — elements, valves, pilot, thermocouple and anode rod are frequently the real cause and cost far less than a new tank. Alfa’s published advice is to check the warranty and diagnose the exact leak point first: a leak at the top is usually a plumbing part, a leak from the body usually means the tank.
Why is my water heater knocking or making noise?
Usually sediment at the bottom of the tank trapping heat under the water. Left alone it destroys a tank from the inside out. A flush fixes most of it, which is why the company publishes an annual maintenance checklist — pressure-relief valve test, temperature at 120°F, flush, element and condensing-unit inspection, and records kept.
What should I do if a pipe is leaking?
Shut the fixture’s stop valve if it has one, otherwise close the main. Open the lowest faucet to drain the line, catch what’s still in the pipe, and photograph the leak before anything is wiped up. Then call — a temporary clamp or epoxy patch on a pressurized line commonly fails, and the water damage behind the wall keeps growing.
How do I know if I have an underground water leak?
Six signs Alfa publishes: the leak indicator on the water meter turning with everything off; a patch of lawn that is greener, spongier or pooling; an unexplained jump in the water bill; new moisture in the slab or crawl space; sinking or heaving soil and cracked pavement; and a whole-house pressure drop, sometimes with an audible hiss near the line. The company checks the meter and uses drain and sewer cameras to pinpoint the spot before digging.
What should I do if I smell gas?
Get everyone — people and pets — outside. Don’t touch light switches, appliances, or your phone inside the house, and extinguish open flames. If it is safe to reach, turn the meter valve to horizontal. From outside or a neighbour’s phone, call your gas utility’s emergency line, then call Alfa. Gas lines, flues and venting are licensed plumbing work; one review describes Alfa correcting an unsafe water heater flue that was surrounded by insulation and bringing the install back within state fire code.
Do you give free estimates, and is financing available?
Yes to both, as published: call for a no-obligation consultation and a free in-home estimate, and financing options are offered for repairs or installations when the budget is limited. New customers get 10% off the first appointment on any service over $300.
