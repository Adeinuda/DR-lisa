Q5

How do I know if I have a plumbing leak?

+

Watch for wall or ceiling stains, sounds of running water, unexplained increases in your water bill, wet spots on flooring or tile, and reduced water pressure. We provide electronic leak detection for water, gas and sewer drain systems.

Q6

When should I call a plumber for a clogged drain?

+

When drains are slow, gurgle, or emit foul odors — and especially when multiple fixtures clog at once, which can indicate a sewer line problem. Preventive drain inspections catch build-up before it becomes costly.

+
